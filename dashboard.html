    Deep dive: EPA over replacement

    This panel ignores the filters above; it always uses the regular season, for the season and position you pick here. Expected points a player added beyond a replacement-level player on the same workload. How it is computed: findings 11 and 12 of the report.

    The starting view is regular season only, all seasons, passing yards per team-game, which matches the report. Tick Playoffs under "More filters" to include them. Counts and totals add up players' stats; rates divide the two sums shown in each measure's definition. Player-season rankings treat each player's season as one row, so nothing is a career total. Per-team-game rates count team-games among the rows in view. Data: nflverse weekly player stats (CC-BY-4.0). This is an unofficial, non-commercial student project.
